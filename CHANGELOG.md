# Changelog

## 0.9.0
- Complete GB blue corporate identity and new app icons.
- Expanded station and metropolitan network model.
- Predictive station autocomplete replaces oversized station menus.
- Urban access and egress legs are automatically added for non-long-distance stops.
- New route generation profiles: fast ICE, IC, regional RE, low-cost RB and night services.
- International routes preserve San Juan del Río border control and RB México.
