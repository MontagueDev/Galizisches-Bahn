# Changelog

## 0.9.1 — Rail Operations Update

- Added a formal operating plan with 46 ICE, IC, RE, RB, RB México and NightJet corridors.
- Added fixed stopping patterns, service frequencies, rolling stock and train-number generation.
- ICE 1 now begins at Puerto Vallarta Hbf and runs through Pazifikhafen, Guadalajara, Löwenstadt and Karlsburg to San Juan del Río Grenzbahnhof.
- Journey planning now prioritizes official corridors and realistic one-transfer combinations.
- Urban U-Bahn/S-Bahn access and egress remain automatic for non-long-distance stops.
- Station departure and arrival boards now use the official operating plan.
- Journey details show corridor names, platforms, rolling stock and frequency.
- Added an official-lines explorer to the national network screen.
- Preserved the mandatory RB México transfer and border control in both directions.
- Rebuilt the downloadable ticket PDF as a structured A4 GB document.
- Updated PWA cache, build metadata and standalone preview.
